Our Story

A Place Built For Serious Students

SR Self Study Zone & Library was founded with one goal: to give every serious student in Shahdol access to a world-class study environment without having to travel to a metro city.

We provide a peaceful, disciplined and distraction-free environment where students can focus completely on their goals — whether it's UPSC, MPPSC, SSC, Banking, Railway, JEE, NEET or any other competitive examination.

From our AC-cooled rooms to our ergonomic chairs, every detail has been thoughtfully designed to maximize your productivity and comfort.
